About Doggie Walks

Doggie Walks is a small dog walking and pet care business based in Lancashire. We're dog people — we genuinely love what we do, and we look after every dog like it's our own.

Why We Do What We Do

We started Doggie Walks because life gets busy and dogs still need their walks. We wanted to make it easy for owners across Lancashire and Merseyside to know their dog is happy and looked after, even on the hectic days.

Happy, Healthy Dogs

Regular walks make a real difference. A tired dog is a happy dog — calmer at home, less likely to cause trouble, and generally in a better mood. We make sure every walk has enough time and space for a proper run around.

How It Works

We collect your dog from your home and take them to a local park for a walk with their pals. With a maximum of 4 dogs per walk, each pet gets proper attention. We also accept drop-offs at your nearest local branch — visit our contact page to find your closest location.
