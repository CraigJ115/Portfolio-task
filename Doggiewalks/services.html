Our Services

We provide professional and personal care for your beloved pets. Our walks are designed to keep your dog happy, active and social.

Dog Walking — £12/hour

Daily dog walks that feel like mini adventures! Whether your pup loves sniffing every tree or power-walking like they've got places to be, each walk is tailored to their energy, pace, and personality. Safety, fun, and plenty of fresh air guaranteed.

Not all dogs enjoy group walks, so we offer both solo and group options — enquire to find out which is best for your dog. Standard duration is 1 hour; longer sessions available on request.

House Visits — £15/hour

Perfect for pets who prefer their own throne (aka your sofa). House visits include feeding, fresh water, cuddles, litter or toilet breaks, and a quick wellbeing check so your pet feels calm and cared for while you're away.

Standard visits are 1 hour. Longer sessions available on request.

Day Care — £60/day

A full day of supervised fun, play, naps, and social time with other friendly pets. Think of it as a mini holiday club where your pet gets attention, stimulation, and a whole lot of tail-wagging energy release.

Day care runs for 8 hours and covers feeding, bathing, walking and sleeping in our clean, up-to-date facilities.

Mon–Fri: 8am–7pm | Sat: 8am–5pm | Sun: 12pm–5pm
