What we offer

Three main services — walking, home visits and day care. Prices are listed below. If you're not sure which one fits, just give us a ring and we'll help you figure it out.

Dog Walking — £12/hour

We take your dog out for a 1 hour walk in a local park or green space. Groups are kept small — max 4 dogs — so your dog gets proper attention rather than just being one of a crowd.

Some dogs prefer going solo, so just let us know and we'll sort that instead. Need longer than an hour? Just ask.

House Visits — £15/hour

We come to your home, check in on your pet, give them a feed, some fuss and a toilet break. Works well for dogs who don't like being moved around, or if you just need someone to pop in during the day.

Visits are 1 hour as standard. Get in touch if you need longer.

Day Care — £60/day

Your dog spends the full day with us — walks, playtime and rest. We cover feeding and bathing too. Good option if you've got a long day and don't want to leave your dog home alone.

Day care runs for 8 hours and costs £60 for the day.

Mon–Fri: 8am–7pm | Sat: 8am–5pm | Sun: 12pm–5pm
