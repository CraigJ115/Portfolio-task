01 Backstory

I'm in my final year of Web Design at Edge Hill. I gravitated towards UX because the psychology behind it genuinely interests me, why do people click the wrong button? Why does a small layout change completely alter someone's behaviour? That's what I want to keep working on.

Before uni I spent six years at Aktrion, worked my way up from general operative to team leader, managing 40+ staff across shifts. That taught me a lot about people, process and how decisions at the top affect everyone else. Useful background to carry into UX.

The AI project has gotten a bit bigger than I originally planned and is now sitting in it's own SSD. It's running four separate models such as Llama 3.1 as the backbone for general stuff, Qwen 2.5 Coder for anything that needs debugging, DeepSeek-R1 when something needs actual reasoning rather than a confident guess, and Gemma2:9b for planning. Less "personal assistant", and more "assembled a small AI team and told them to figure it out." It's going well until one of them change a bit of code and break the whole thing (Which has happened twice so far..)

Outside of that: big Resident Evil fan, currently RE6 for the first time in years. Also rewatching Supernatural — I've lost count of how many times at this point, probably like 6th or 7th it is basically my comfort show. Musically I'm mostly into classic rock and heavy metal like Black Sabbath, Metallica, AC/DC, that kind of thing.
