Doggie Walks

Dog walking service website

6 pages One stylesheet WCAG AA Scroll animation

01 About the Project

A website built for a fictional dog walking service based across Lancashire and Merseyside. The brief asked for a fully functional multi-page site built from scratch, with a focus on accessibility.

02 What I Built

Six pages: Home, About, Services, Gallery, Meet the Team and Contact, all sharing one stylesheet. I built a sticky header, a services section with pricing, a photo gallery, team profiles and a contact form with validated inputs. I also added a scroll-triggered shiba inu gif that runs up and down the page which I'm pretty happy with.

03 What I Learned

This was the first time I really got into accessibility properly. I learned about WCAG contrast ratios, skip links, ARIA labels and proper semantic HTML. It made me realise how much extra thought goes into building something that actually works for everyone.
