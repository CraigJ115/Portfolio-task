Doggie Walks

Dog walking service website

About the Project

Doggie Walks is a multi-page website built for a fictional dog walking service operating across Lancashire and Merseyside. The brief was to design and build a fully functional, accessible website from scratch.

What I Built

The site includes six pages — Home, About, Services, Gallery, Meet the Team, and Contact — all sharing a single stylesheet. I built a responsive layout with a sticky header and navigation, a services section with pricing cards, a photo gallery, team member profiles, and a contact form with validated inputs. I also added a fun scroll-triggered shiba inu animation throughout the site.

What I Learned

This project gave me a strong grounding in UX-led development — making deliberate decisions about colour contrast (WCAG AA), typography, skip links, ARIA labels and form labelling so the site works for all users, not just the majority. It reinforced that accessibility isn't a checklist but a design discipline: every choice about colour, layout and interaction has a user impact.
