Method

01
Understand

Figure out who's actually using something and where it goes wrong. What people actually do matters more than what you think they'll do.

→
02
Design for Usability

Wireframes and prototypes built around how people actually behave, not how you expect them to. Try to sort the obvious issues before anyone has to deal with them.

→
03
Test & Refine

People say one thing and do another, so I test with real users and go from there.
